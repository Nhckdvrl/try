# 1. V13 formal update — PD

## PD — Partition Dependence / Representation-Induced Probability Prior

**KILL — DIRECTER 2026 PARTITION/STATISTICAL-CONSISTENCY COLLISION.**

The attractive question:
> Can the same event receive different probability merely because the rest of the outcome space is partitioned differently?

was non-obvious, quantitative, and supported by human D2 materials.

However, later search found 2026 work directly studying LLM statistical self-consistency under partitions/aggregation:
> **Partition, Prompt, Aggregate: Statistical Self-Consistency in Language Models**

Together with choice-set, prompt-framing, and confidence-distortion literature, the remaining support-theory/ignorance-prior cell is exact-cell novelty.

Do not resurrect through:
- alternative partition examples;
- support-theory terminology;
- candidate-set framing;
- more precise 1/M predictions.
