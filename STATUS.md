## What V7 changed

1. The zero/nonzero anomaly is no longer a live paper-identity candidate. The raw
   0-vs-1% interaction is not dismissed, but the formal zero-specific law is not
   cross-model stable and the strongest effects are concentrated in small
   task-family skeleton sets.
2. G12→G15 is explicitly recorded as a serious mechanistic asset: unrelated
   verdict-redacted future outcome evidence causes a large recipient-judgment shift
   in Gemma and is converted into a fresh-confirmed recipient-conditioned late
   causal decision coordinate. The behavioral effect is heterogeneous across the
   panel, and nearby 2025–2026 decision-subspace / anchoring / interference work
   prevents promotion to a paper identity.
3. The broad hypothetical-world / mental-space idea is killed before generation:
   2026 work directly studies a shared causal router/value-slot mechanism across
   reality, belief, fiction, memory, temporal, hypothetical, modal and reported
   spaces.
4. The current best *search shape* asks whether models condition evidential value
   on **how evidence was generated, selected, censored, or could have been
   observed**. This has not passed the novelty/scale gate and is **not** a pilot.

## Pilot gate

Before any new generation, a candidate must pass all six:

1. measurement reality;
2. behavioral plausibility / meaningful family and substrate breadth;
3. abstraction test;
4. conceptual novelty assassination against current literature;
5. C1→C2→C3 scale / growth-path test;
6. simple-data / Figure-1 test.

No current candidate has passed all six.
